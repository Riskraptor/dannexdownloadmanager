# Website: dannex.kavangi.co.ke

Scope: every page `tools/website/build.py` makes. Home, download, IDM alternative and
benchmark are Persuade; help, FAQ, news and legal pages are Read and take the same world with
restraint. 21 languages, Arabic and Persian right to left; light and dark. (The browser
extension's own pages are another agent's, to match this world.)

Visitor: an IDM user, often non-technical, looking for a free replacement that works the same.
Action: Download (Windows installer from GitHub Releases). Proof: the real app window, the
public benchmark, the price of nothing. Constraint: clean room, no IDM artwork; no invented
claims; every image is the real app. Identity: the Comet icon (Night #1E2A66, Night deep
#070A1E, Head #FFF7E6, Amber #FFB23E, Ember #FF6A45, Plum tail #B43A8C), the owner's choice.

Memorable moment: the white-hot comet striking the real DDM window, printed on a night-ink label.

## Direction contract

THESIS: DDM is sold the way a sky-shot is: on its printed packet label. The site is that label
in five spot inks, the Comet's, so the icon, the installer and the page are one print job. It
refuses the category default (dark hero, glowing screenshot, rows of icon cards) and the waybill
it replaces: no board, no paper sheets, no shadows under text.

OWN-WORLD: White label stock (night-deep stock in dark) printed in Night, Amber, Ember, Plum and
Head. Night fields own the hero, the race and the close, framed in a double amber rule with a
four-point spark at each corner; lighter labels are stock framed in a night double rule. Archivo
expanded and heavy for billing, plain for reading, tabular figures for every measure. Print is
flat: frames and rules separate, only the real window casts a shadow. Ember by law: the Download
action and arrival only; plum for links; amber for field names and frames on night. Raises, by
donor: lines stack by billing and size alone carries rank (zoot handbill); every fact line ends in
its measure, end-aligned (cassette j-card); annotations are pinned to what they name by a leader
(tensegrity column); in each view only the live thing takes the hot inks (brick instructions);
states differ by mark, never by colour alone (one-bit desktop); night ink owns at least a third
of every Persuade page taken as a whole, its fields alternating with stock, not of every screen
(cutting bench; the project lead's reading, 2026-11-23: the light theme stays light).

STORY: The visitor sees the window they know struck by a comet on a label, reads that it is
rebuilt, faster and free, watches the two shots race to the real times, and downloads.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One night-ink label the full content width, double amber rule, a spark at each
corner. Start side, stacked by billing: the maker line (logo, name, free and open source), the
headline in expanded heavy Head ink with an ember impression a hair off register, the subline,
the ember Download split button, macOS and Linux, the extension line. End side: the real window
(light or dark screenshot) printed into the panel and resting on the label's foot, the comet's
three streaks falling from the top into a white-hot head on the window's top end corner, the
caption pinned to the window by an amber leader. Along the foot, under a double amber rule, the
contents strip: postage, delivery time, system, version, and the release's barcode on a Head
patch. Under the label, on stock: "Made possible by these amazing humans" and the contributors'
row. Phone: the same label, one column: maker, window with the comet, billing, action,
contents, barcode; the crew below. Header slim on stock, a night double rule under it.

FORM: grounded candidate 7 of 7 (the firework packet label, offset-printed in spot inks);
seed:bebe339e50c32938. Signature interaction: the race as two shots fired at the real benchmark
times, each lane a dashed fuse that becomes the comet's streak with its head where the bytes are
arriving, an amber seal landing on Delivered; replayable. Motion grammar: the hero's comet falls in once and stops; the race moves damped in real time;
the comparison's ours-row fills once as its seal lands, then rests; nothing else animates on
entry; reduced motion shows every finish at rest.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
